Lesson

About 5 minutes.

1. Why we screen

Substance use is common but under-recognized in primary care, and patients rarely raise it themselves. In the Saudi National Mental Health Survey, about 4% of adults had a substance use disorder at some point in their lives, and fewer than half sought treatment. Two questions take less than a minute and find people who would otherwise go unseen.

  • Early detection opens the door to help before harm builds up.
  • Brief counseling works: it reduces unhealthy drinking in adults.
  • No one feels singled out when every adult is asked the same way.
  • It's recommended: USPSTF advises screening adults for unhealthy alcohol use (2018) and drug use (2020), Grade B.
  • Ask every adult once a year, at any visit, or sooner if there is clinical concern (for example injuries, repeated sick leave, or requests for tramadol or pregabalin).

2. The questions

Use the exact wording. The accuracy of the screen depends on it.

Q2 Drugs or medicines (ask everyone)"How many times in the past year have you used an illegal drug or used a prescription medication for nonmedical reasons?"في آخر ١٢ شهر، كم مرة استخدمت مادة مخدرة، أو أخذت دواء يحتاج وصفة طبية بس مو عشان العلاج؟ (مثلاً عشان الإحساس أو الراحة اللي يعطيك)

3. The number in Q1: 4 or 5

Q1 counts days of heavy drinking. The number that counts as heavy depends on the patient:

PatientAsk about
Men under 655 or more drinks in a day
Women (any age)4 or more drinks in a day
Men 65 and older4 or more drinks in a day

One drink is about 14 g of alcohol: a can of beer, a glass of wine, or a single shot of spirits.

4. What counts as positive

Both questions ask how many times. You only need to know: zero, or one or more?

QuestionAnswerResult
Pre-screenNoAlcohol negative. Go to Q2
Pre-screenYesNot positive yet. Ask Q1
Q1Never (0)Alcohol negative
Q1Once, 3 times, every weekendPositive
Q2Never (0)Drugs negative
Q2Once, a few timesPositive
The rule: if Q1 or Q2 is 1 or more, the screen is positive. One positive answer is enough to offer Azm triage.

"Nonmedical reasons" means taking a prescription medicine for the feeling it gives, or without a prescription, for example tramadol or pregabalin.

5. How to ask

Start with a sentence that reassures the patient:

"I ask all my patients these two questions about alcohol and medications. It's routine, and it's for your health and safety. Your answers stay in your medical record and have no consequences for your work or any legal matter."pending legal confirmation"نسأل كل مراجعينا سؤالين عن الكحول وبعض الأدوية، وهذا شي روتيني لصحتك وسلامتك. إجاباتك تبقى في ملفك الطبي، وما يترتب عليها أي شي يخص عملك أو أي أمور قانونية."

Do

  • Ask in private, without companions
  • Use the exact wording
  • Keep a calm, neutral tone
  • Thank the patient for being honest

Avoid

  • Asking in front of others
  • "Are you using something?"
  • Comments or blame after the answer
  • Rushing to refer without explaining

6. If the screen is positive

  1. Thank the patient and say: "I'd like to book you an appointment at the family medicine clinic in Azm, in person or by phone. This is for your health and safety, and it has no consequences for your work or any legal matter. Is that OK with you?"pending legal confirmation"أبغى أحجز لك موعد في عيادة طب الأسرة في مركز عزم، حضوري أو عن بعد. هذا لصحتك وسلامتك، وما يترتب عليه أي شي يخص عملك أو أي أمور قانونية. يناسبك؟"
  2. If they agree: book Azm triage in TrakCare before they leave the clinic.
  3. If they decline: respect it, document it, and give them the Azm QR code for direct contact.
  4. Red flags at any visit (severe withdrawal, intoxication, suicidal risk, psychosis): send to the Security Forces Hospital ER the same day.
